# How to Refresh Evergreen TikTok Content When Your Product Changes

Evergreen TikTok content can become inaccurate while its topic remains useful. A tutorial about opening your template still answers a real question, but the screen, included files, or price may have changed since you recorded it.

Review the change against your content inventory before reusing an old video. Decide which material remains accurate, which needs replacement, and which you should stop reusing.

This is a maintenance process for content promoting your own product. It does not assume that Groniz can automatically edit or delete an existing TikTok post.

## Describe the product change precisely

Consider a hypothetical creator selling a downloadable client-onboarding pack. Version 1 includes an intake form and a welcome-email template. Version 2 adds a project checklist, renames one form section, and changes the listed price.

The creator lists each change before searching the video archive. "Updated the pack" is too broad to guide a review. A useful change note identifies the old value, new value, effective date, and current evidence.

For the price, that evidence is the current offer and checkout configuration. For the renamed section, it is the released file. For the new checklist, it is the package a buyer now receives.

The creator still counts paid pack purchases in checkout records. Updating a video keeps its product details accurate, but measuring a sales increase takes separate evidence. Keep that distinction from the [TikTok automation and owned-offer model](https://groniz.com/blog/how-to-make-money-by-automating-your-tiktok) visible in the review.

## Search by claims as well as filenames

A file called `welcome-tip.mp4` might contain the old price in its closing frame. Search captions, scripts, briefs, and approval notes, then inspect the actual export. Review spoken narration and on-screen text, including the first and last frames.

Include draft, approved, scheduled, and already published material in the inventory. What you do next depends on the video's status. An unfinished edit can be corrected before approval; a published post requires a decision based on the destination's currently available controls.

Also inspect the linked destination. A video may remain accurate while its caption sends people to an outdated product page. Record that as a destination issue even if no new filming is needed.

A [versioned video file structure](https://groniz.com/blog/how-to-organize-tiktok-video-files-for-automated-publishing) helps identify the source project behind a published export. Without that reference, a small screen change may become a complete remake.

## Match each change to the affected videos

For the onboarding pack, the creator finds four hypothetical videos:

| Video | Product dependency | What changed | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| ON011, package tour | Exact included files | Checklist added | Replace the tour so it shows the current package |
| ON012, intake walkthrough | Visible form labels | One section renamed | Re-record affected screen segment and revise narration if needed |
| ON013, price explanation | Spoken and displayed price | Listed price changed | Hold future reuse; create a newly reviewed version |
| ON014, writing a welcome message | General example using unchanged email template | No relevant change | Keep after checking its caption and destination |

Mark a video "keep" only after checking the actual content against the product changes. Its filename alone may miss a claim that needs updating.

Consider what each change means for someone following the video. A color change may be harmless if the instruction is still easy to follow. A changed field name can matter if the viewer must locate that field. A changed price affects expectations even when the rest of the demonstration remains accurate.

## Choose the replacement scope

Use the smallest replacement that preserves a coherent, accurate video. A screen pickup may be enough for ON012. ON011 needs a new package tour because its central answer has changed. ON013 needs new spoken and on-screen pricing, plus a caption review.

Keep existing approved versions unchanged. Save a new export and record which earlier version it supersedes. A substantive content change needs review again before the replacement enters a delivery handoff.

For future planned deliveries, flag affected items immediately so the person handling delivery can check their current state and act using the route's available controls. For published posts, inspect the current platform controls and decide whether a supported correction, removal, or replacement is appropriate. Record what was actually done. Keep the item open until you have checked the result on the live post.

## Use a refresh record for each affected video

```text
Product change reference and effective date:
Video ID and export version:
Current state and publication reference, if any:
Affected spoken claim or on-screen detail:
Affected caption or destination:
Evidence for the new value:
Buyer consequence if left unchanged:
Decision: keep / revise / replace / retire from reuse
Required recording or editing work:
New export and caption versions:
Review owner and decision:
Delivery action needed:
Verified action supported by chosen route:
Action taken and evidence:
Next review trigger:
```

These are fields in your own maintenance record. Groniz does not perform this claim audit or approve the replacement.

## Close the review with evidence

To close the review, give every affected video a decision and an owner, and record the outcome of each required delivery action. If an old video is still live and its correction remains unresolved, keep that item open in the record.

Set the next review trigger around the product: another file release, offer change, renamed control, or revised requirement. You can also choose periodic checks, but a calendar date should not delay an urgent factual correction.

When a replacement export and caption are approved, [open Groniz Connectors](https://groniz.com/console/connectors) to prepare supported delivery. Link the new delivery receipt to the refresh record so the next reviewer can find which version replaced the old video and why.
